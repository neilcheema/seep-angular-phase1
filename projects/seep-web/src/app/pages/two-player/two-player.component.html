How to play
  • Capture values: A=1, 2-10 face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13.
  • Play a card and select floor cards/houses whose total equals its value to capture them.
  • Build a house (value 9-13) from your card plus loose floor cards — you need another card of that value left in hand.
  • Cement a house by adding a card (optionally with loose floor cards) whose total is a multiple of the house's value — you need another card of that value left in hand afterward.
  • Select an existing house to break it up to a higher value instead, using a card (optionally with loose floor cards) that doesn't total a multiple of its current value.
  • You must capture if a capture is available — you can only throw a card down loose otherwise.
  • Clearing the whole floor is a sweep: worth 50 points (25 on the opening move, 0 on the last card).
  • Spades score their value, other aces score 1, the Ten of Diamonds scores 6. Under 9 points for a hand scores 0.
  • First player to lead by 100 points wins the match.
