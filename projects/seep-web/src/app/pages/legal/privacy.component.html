{{ legal.siteName }} ({{ legal.siteAddress }}) is a free card game. This page explains what information we handle when you use it, why, who else can see it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it.

The short version
  • You can play against the computer without an account. Those games run in your browser and the moves are not sent to us.
  • To play online with other people you sign in. We then keep your email address, the name you choose, and a record of the games you play.
  • The people you play with see your chosen name and the moves of the games you share. They do not see your email address.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from Play online.

Who we are

{{ legal.siteName }} is run by {{ legal.operator }}. You can reach us about anything on this page at {{ legal.contactEmail }}.

What we collect

When you sign in

Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service from Google. If you create an account with an email address, we and Google hold that address, and Google holds a protected form of your password (we never see your password). If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your email address. Google's sign-in service keeps what it needs to run your account, which may include your name and photo from your Google profile. Our own database keeps your email address and your chosen display name, not your Google name or photo. (We may use the first word of your Google name to suggest a display name when you first choose one.)

The name you choose

To play online you choose a display name. It is shown to the people you play with.

Your games

When you play online we keep a record of the tables you create or join, which seat you took, every move made, the cards dealt, the scores and the result. This is how the game works: your opponent's screen is built from the same record.

Technical records

Our hosting providers keep standard technical logs of requests to the website and the game server, such as the time, the page or action requested, any error, and the network (IP) address it came from. We use them to keep the service running, to find faults, and to protect it from abuse. They are kept for a limited time and are not used for advertising.

Playing against the computer

Games against the computer run entirely in your browser and are not sent to us. (Your browser still asks our hosting provider for the page itself, which appears in the technical records above.)

If you email us

We receive your message and your email address, and keep them for as long as we need to deal with it.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and seat you at a table.
  • To run games by the rules, including the turn clock and forfeits.
  • To show your opponents your display name.
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your display name and the moves and results of the tables you share with them. They do not see your email address.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: Google (Firebase Authentication, and Google sign-in if you use it), Microsoft Azure (hosting for the website and game server, and technical logs), and Neon (hosting for our database, which runs on Amazon Web Services). They handle information only to provide those services to us.
  • Others, if the law requires it, or to protect the safety and security of people or the service.

Where it is kept

Our game server and its logs are hosted in the western United States (Microsoft Azure, West US 2). Our database is hosted by Neon on Amazon Web Services in Oregon, United States. Google's sign-in service runs on Google's own infrastructure and may handle your sign-in details in other countries too. If you are outside the United States, online play means your information is handled in the United States, where privacy laws, and the access that courts and authorities have, may differ from where you live.

How long we keep it

  • Your account (email address and display name): until you delete your account.
  • Records of games: until about {{ legal.retention.deleteAfterDays }} days after a game ends or is closed. A table that nobody has played at or looked at for {{ legal.retention.abandonAfterDays }} days is closed first, so a game record normally disappears about {{ weeks }} weeks after anyone last used it.
  • After you delete your account, nothing that identifies you stays, except an anonymous account number, kept for {{ legal.retention.deletionMarkerHours }} hours so that a sign-in which was still open cannot bring the account back. Games you were in remain for the other players, without your name, until they expire as above.
  • Technical records: a limited time, set by our hosting providers' settings, used only to run and protect the service.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need to deal with them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account yourself: go to Play online and choose Delete my account. This forfeits any match in progress (so your opponent gets a clean result), frees your seat at tables still waiting for players (or closes them if nobody else is there), removes your email address and name from our database, and deletes your sign-in record at Google.
  • Change your name any time from Play online.
  • Ask us at {{ legal.contactEmail }} to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or about anything in this policy. We will answer as quickly as we can, and within the time the law requires.
  • Complain if you are not satisfied: you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

Security

Connections to {{ legal.siteName }} use HTTPS, and your sign-in is checked on every request. Access to our systems is limited to the people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed in, Google's sign-in service stores a sign-in record in your browser. Signing out removes it, and you can also clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top and, for important changes, tell signed-in players on the site.
