How to play (team rules)
  • Capture values: A=1, 2-10 face value, J=11, Q=12, K=13.
  • You (Player {{ seatNumber(mySeat()) }}) and Player {{ seatNumber(partnerSeat()) }} are partners (Team {{ myTeamLetter() }}) against Players {{ seatNumber(leftSeat()) }} and {{ seatNumber(rightSeat()) }} (Team {{ otherTeamLetter() }}) — captured cards are pooled by team, not by seat.
  • Bidding and the opening move are decided from just the bidder's first four cards and the four-card floor — everyone else's hands aren't dealt until right after the opening move.
  • Build a house (value 9-13) from your card plus loose floor cards — you need another card of that value left in hand.
  • A house can be cemented by adding any combination — a card from hand, plus optional loose floor cards — whose total is a multiple of the house's value, even at the moment you found it, combining several complete sets into one house that's cemented from the start.
  • You can only found a new house for yourself, never on your partner's behalf.
  • You can't break a house you already own — a teammate or opponent has to break it instead. Breaking makes you the new owner, unless the new value matches a house your partner already holds, in which case the two merge into one cemented team house.
  • Adding a matching card to your partner's house is free — no reserve card needed. Your own or an opponent's house still needs one.
  • You must capture if a capture is available with the card you play. If there's more than one exact match on the floor — a house plus one or more separate loose groups all worth the same value — you must take all of them together in one move, not just one.
  • A house is only captured by a card matching its own exact value — its value is never combined with loose cards to reach some other total.
  • Clearing the whole floor is a sweep: 50 points normally, 25 if it happens on the very first move of the hand, 0 if it's the very last card played in the hand.
  • Spades score their face value, other-suit aces score 1 point, and the Ten of Diamonds scores 6 — everything else is worth 0. Under 9 points in a hand scores 0.
  • First team to lead by 100 points wins the match.
  • The dealer's team deals again if they're behind or tied after a hand; once they're ahead, the deal passes to the next seat.
  • Every move — yours and {{ opponentPossessive() }} — pauses so you can check it before play continues.
